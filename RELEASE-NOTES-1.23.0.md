# Chalant 1.23.0

The strip is built for speed.

## Slipstream

Hold Option and the strip is new. Lower and tauter, with machined corners in
place of the soft pill, and it opens the instant you press. Near dark while
you are silent; speaking turns it vivid. Every syllable ticks the edge
brighter the moment it lands and sends a pair of glints racing the top edge,
a pool of your accent stirs inside the glass, and the strip reads how fast
you are talking: speak quickly and it moves quickly with you, slow down and
it composes itself. Letting go fires your words up into the notch.

## The words land once

What you say lands once, already tidy, and stays. No more watching a
sentence change after it arrived. When tidying is not ready in time, your
words land exactly as you said them, and stay that way too. The ear also
keeps itself warm for ten minutes after a hold, so the next one answers at
once.

# Chalant 1.23.2

The strip is smaller, and it is alive.

## Smaller, and off the notch

The dictation strip is now one small floating shape on every screen: 260 wide
and 28 tall, with the same machined corners everywhere. On a MacBook it sits
just beneath the notch instead of wrapping it, whatever island style you
chose; the notch stays hardware.

## You can see it listening

The light inside the glass now lives: a pool of your accent drifts and
breathes the whole time the strip is listening, gently while you pause, and
faster and wider the more voice it hears. The edge and the pool answer every
syllable, the glints are easier to catch, and the whole response is brighter
under your voice while silence stays dark. It moves in place; nothing slides.

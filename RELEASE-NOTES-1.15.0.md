# Chalant 1.15.0

You can see it listening now.

## The island breathes with your voice

Hold left Option and the notch grows into a low black strip. There is no
meter drawn on it. The strip is the meter: its silver edge and a pool of
light at its base swell with how loud you are, and one small dot at the
centre grows with them. Talk and it brightens. Pause and it settles. Let
go and it closes.

It opens on the screen showing the app you are dictating into, because
that is where your eyes are. Not on your main display, not wherever the
pointer happens to be.

The old floating box under the notch is gone. It appeared on the wrong
screen, disappeared in under a second, and was dark on a dark notch, which
is why holding the key felt like nothing was happening.

## What is on it, and what is not

The app your words are going into on the left. The dot in the middle.
Which microphone is live on the right. That is the whole strip.

No words while you talk. No finish hint. Letting go is the instruction.

## Silence looks like silence

If the strip opens and never lights up, your microphone is delivering
nothing. A closed lid does that to a MacBook. Before, you would have
dictated into a dead mic and only found out when no text arrived. Now you
can see it before you say a word.

## Smaller things

Dictating while the island is already open works. Before, holding the key
with the island open showed nothing while the text still landed.

An agent finishing while you are mid-dictation no longer opens the island
over the strip, and no longer leaves your music paused afterwards.

The voice-command hotkey and the mic buttons refuse to start a second
listener while you are dictating.

# Chalant 1.34.0

The second ear starts teaching.

## A word misheard twice is fixed from then on

Chalant listens a second time after your words land, with a slower,
more careful ear, and until now it could only use what it heard where
replacing text is safe. In your editor it never was, so the better
hearing was thrown away.

Now nothing is wasted. When the second ear hears a word differently
from what landed, Chalant remembers the pair. Hear the same pair
twice and the fix moves to the front of the line: from then on the
word lands right the first time, in every app, with nothing changing
on your screen after the fact.

It stays careful about it. Only words the microphone itself was unsure
of can be replaced, everyday English is never rewritten, and a pair
you delete stays deleted.

# Chalant 1.10.1

Settings got shorter. Eight pages are now six, and every page answers one question.

## What moved

Three pages were all answering "what appears, and where", and two of them even had a card with the same name. They are one page now.

- **Glance** and **Arrangement** are gone as pages. Everything they held lives on **What shows**, which now reads top to bottom: what to show or hide, where each block sits in the island, your presets, then what appears beside the notch when the island is closed, which one wins that space, and what is allowed to interrupt.
- **General** lost a card: the two "Opening" groups were one topic, so they are one card.

## What did not move

Nothing was removed. Every switch, picker, slider, drag row and button is still there, in the same words, remembering the same thing it remembered before. This is a reorganization, not a cut.

## Also

The date on your day is quieter. It was wearing the same weight as a song title, which made it the loudest thing on the island while saying the least.

## Under the hood

600 tests, unchanged.

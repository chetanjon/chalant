# Chalant 1.27.0

The campaign's first verdicts, all in your favor.

## Your words land the moment you stop talking

The measuring stick 1.26.0 built has delivered its first ruling: the
cleanup model was not earning the wait. So the wait is gone. Words now
land instantly, exactly as you said them, and the model studies the
transcript afterwards, off the path, its opinions recorded for the
corpus instead of standing between you and your document. And the wait
that remained in a few stubborn cases, where a 0.73 second budget
somehow cost 2.5 seconds, is fixed at the root: the budget is now a
real deadline, and it always returns on time.

## Say "no wait" and it just works

Self-corrections resolve as you speak, deterministically, with no model
involved: "forty units, scratch that, fifty" lands as "fifty";
"send it to Priya, I mean Rohan" lands as "send it to Rohan". On the
recorded correction corpus this cut the correction noise in half. The
transcriber's trailing pause dots also settle into the punctuation you
could have meant: a period before a capital, a comma before the
sentence goes on.

## The ear stops following ghosts

Your iPhone can no longer volunteer itself as the microphone: phones
appear briefly, steal the ear, then vanish mid-sentence, so they are
exiled from automatic choice. And when the microphone you were using
disappears (a cable pulled, earphones unplugged), Chalant now rebuilds
onto a working ear within a second, announced, instead of feeding
silence to a dead device. The cleanup model also warms itself the
instant you press the key, so dictating after a long pause no longer
pays a cold-start penalty at the one moment you are waiting.

## This one update installs by hand

A signing key had to be replaced this release, so 1.26.0 cannot verify
this update on its own: download 1.27.0 once from the site and install
it over your copy. Every version after this updates itself again, as
before.

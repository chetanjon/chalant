# Chalant 1.17.1

Instant by default. And a smaller, lighter strip.

## Your words, the moment you let go

Dictation no longer waits for the on-device tidy pass. Words land about a
tenth of a second after you release Option, exactly as you said them, with the
ums, uhs and doubled words already taken out.

The tidy pass (fillers gone, punctuation and grammar fixed, on this Mac) is
now a switch: Settings, General, Dictation, "Tidy what I said". It adds about
a second before your words appear, which is why it is off unless you want it.

## The strip, lighter

While you hold Option, the strip is now 320 wide and only as tall as your
screen's notch needs, and it is dark glass instead of solid black. Its edge
lights the instant you speak, spreads as you keep talking, and a point of
light rises into the notch when you let go.

## Under the hood

The strip's light is drawn on Core Animation layers now. Holding the key used
to cost a third of a processor core; it costs a tenth of that.

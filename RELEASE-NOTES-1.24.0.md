# Chalant 1.24.0

It hears you better, writes it cleaner, and never loses a word.

## The second ear pulls its weight

With Better hearing on, the Whisper ear now listens on every dictation and
starts the moment your words finalize, so its corrections land even when you
dictate sentence after sentence without a pause.

## Fewer commas, and a repeated thought lands once

Speech-to-text drops a comma wherever you pause; Chalant now clears the ones
that were only pauses, in the tidy pass and in its own cleanup. And when you
say the same thing twice, or restart a sentence and finish it differently,
you get one clean version: the one you finished with. A repair that would
change your meaning is never made; those land exactly as you said them.

## Nowhere to type? Nothing is lost

Hold Option and speak with no text field picked, on the desktop, on a button,
and your words go to the clipboard instead of vanishing, with the island
telling you so: press paste wherever you meant to be. A password field or a
focus change mid-dictation now says so too. The apps where dictation already
works are untouched.

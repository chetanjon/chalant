# Chalant 1.18.0

Instant, then tidied in place. And spoken lists become lists.

## Your words land at once, and get tidied a moment later

Dictation stays instant: your words appear about a tenth of a second after
you let go, exactly as you said them. Then, about a second later, the on-device
tidy pass replaces them in place with the cleaned version: ums and false starts
gone, punctuation and small slips fixed. Only if it changed something, and only
if you have not started typing or clicked since. If you have moved on, nothing
is touched.

For a long paragraph, the tidying starts while you are still talking, so the
wait at the end stays short.

Where the swap could go wrong, it does not happen: in terminals, and in editors
that carry a terminal (VS Code, Cursor, Zed), you get the instant text and it is
left alone.

## Say a list, get a list

"Three things: first… second… third" comes back as bullets. "Number one, number
two, number three" comes back numbered. "New paragraph" breaks the line. Names,
numbers and every "not" are still checked afterwards.

## Smaller things

- A word the microphone stalls on ("the, the, the, the") is collapsed to one.
- "Tidy what I said" is on by default again, now that it costs you no waiting;
  the switch stays in Settings, General, Dictation.

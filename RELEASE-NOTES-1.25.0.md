# Chalant 1.25.0

One app, your call.

## What should Chalant be?

On Macs that can dictate, Chalant now asks up front: Both, Just dictation,
or The island. Choose Just dictation and the island disappears entirely: no
hovering it open, no hotkeys, nothing at the top of your screen until you
hold Option and talk. The strip appears while you speak, the glance tells
you when your words went to the clipboard, updates still find you, and
nothing else does. Choose The island and dictation simply stays off.

Change your mind any time in Settings, Dictation, "Chalant is."

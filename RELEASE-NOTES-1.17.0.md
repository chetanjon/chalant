# Chalant 1.17.0

The dictation strip's edge is the light.

## Hold Option, and the island listens

While you hold left Option and talk, the low strip under the notch no longer
carries a dot and two grey labels. Its own outline glows: the light rises the
instant you speak and settles softly when you stop, and the lit stretch of
edge spreads from the centre outward the longer you keep talking. Pause and
it eases back a little. Let go, and a small point of light rises into the
notch as the strip closes: sent.

The light takes the same colour as the island's accent, silver by default and
the album's colour when music is playing.

## What is gone

The centre dot, the pool of light at the base, and the microphone's name.
Only the name of the app your words are going into stays on the strip, quiet,
bottom left. A dead microphone is still plain to see: the edge sits at its
dim resting glow and never blooms.

# Chalant 1.20.1

The microphone rests.

## No mic, no power, when you are not dictating

Chalant used to keep the microphone open all the time so a hold could start
with no delay. That kept your Mac's mic hardware busy all day and the orange
mic dot lit in the menu bar. Now the ear sleeps until you press Option, wakes
in about a tenth of a second the first time, stays warm for three minutes
after your last dictation, and then closes the mic again. Idle Chalant holds
no microphone and asks nothing of the battery.

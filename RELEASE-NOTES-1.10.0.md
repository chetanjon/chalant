# Chalant 1.10.0

The island got its finish. Everything you see is quieter, better spaced, and moves as one piece of liquid. Nothing was added to learn.

## The look

- **Air instead of lines.** The dividers inside the island are gone; space does the separating now.
- **Nothing wears a pill.** The tab you are on, the sound that is playing, the setting that is on: all of them simply turn white. No capsules, no highlight bubbles.
- **The music zone reads like a record sleeve.** Bigger title over a quieter artist, a bare transport, and a full-width progress line with the time under it.
- **Ambience is words.** Brown, Pink, Rain, Fire, Cafe. Tap one to play it; its volume appears only while it is playing.
- **Weather on your day.** The temperature and sky sit beside the date, from a keyless service, only if you allow location. There is a switch for it in Settings, What shows.

## The liquid

The island now pours between sizes on a single curve: opening, closing, and switching tabs are all one continuous motion, with the content arriving a beat behind the shape. It obeys your Feel setting and the system's Reduce Motion.

## Settings and the first hello

The Settings window speaks the same language: no boxed cards, quiet group labels, hairline rows, and white where something is on. The welcome tour matches. Nothing moved, so nothing has to be relearned.

## Fixed

- **The crash notice says itself once.** Dismissing it now counts as having seen it, so an old crash cannot greet you at every launch.
- **Seeking holds.** Dragging the progress line no longer snaps back for a moment while the player catches up.
- **Volume sliders slimmed** to match the island's own line, in both the music and ambience rows.
- **One clock.** The countdown beside the elapsed time is gone.
- **A quiet empty day.** No more "Clear water. Nothing due."

## Under the hood

- 600 tests, up from 587.
- The album's own color pools softly behind the music while it plays, and stops when it stops.

## Privacy

Weather is the only thing that leaves this Mac, and only when you switch it on: an approximate area asked of Open-Meteo, with no account and no key. Everything else Chalant reads stays here.

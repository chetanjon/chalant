Long paragraphs get their corrections too.

## The better ear keeps up with a long thought

With Better hearing on, a long dictation now gets the same second look a
short one always had. The ear's correction window grows with how long you
spoke, so a minute of thinking out loud self-corrects a few seconds after
landing instead of being left as heard. Every safeguard still applies: the
moment you type, click, or switch apps, nothing changes on the page.

# Chalant 1.26.0

The campaign for the best English dictation begins.

## The row tells the truth

Chalant now keeps an honest account of every dictation it handles: whether
your words really landed already-tidied or only looked that way, how much
was cleaned while you were still talking, whether the model started cold,
how the microphone answered the moment you pressed the key, and what the
second ear decided afterwards. Nothing about how dictation feels changes
in this release. It is the measuring stick for everything that comes next:
the speed work, hands-free dictation, and rewriting selected text by
voice, each judged by these numbers instead of by impressions.

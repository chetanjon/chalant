# Chalant 1.10.4

The pill learns the menu bar's manners.

## Out of the way of fullscreen

On a display with no notch, the resting pill used to float over a fullscreen app's own toolbar, a black bar sitting on your tabs. Now, when a window's chrome is directly under the pill's home (a fullscreen app, either half of a split view, a full-height window with the menu bar hidden), the pill steps aside on its own. Push your pointer to the top edge and it comes right back, exactly like the menu bar in fullscreen, and it returns for good the moment the screen opens up again.

Anything urgent still shows itself regardless: a toast, a timer ending, something that needs you. An open island never hides. And a display with a real notch is untouched, since fullscreen apps already keep clear of the housing.

The pill itself is unchanged. A pill you chose stays a pill; it just knows when to step aside.

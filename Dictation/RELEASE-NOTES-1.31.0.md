# Chalant 1.31.0

Polished words, at the speed of raw ones.

## Cleanup lands with your words, or gets out of the way

Chalant cleans each sentence while you are still speaking the next
one, and at release it now makes a fair call: when the cleaned text is
genuinely a breath away, it waits up to one second and lands your
words polished, with their full stops, once. When the cleanup has no
real chance, your words land instantly, exactly as you said them, with
zero wait. The old middle ground, waiting and then landing raw anyway,
is gone.

# Chalant 1.23.1

The strip pops open and holds still.

## The press is a pop

Hold Option and the strip no longer slides open out of the pill: it pops,
there and ready before your first word. Letting go still closes on the
island's smooth pour.

## Nothing moves on its own

The resting sheen that swept across the strip is gone, and so is the subtle
stretch the strip did while you spoke, which could shimmer. At rest the strip
is dark, taut, and completely still. The body of the strip never moves on its
own now; only the light answers your voice.

# Chalant 1.32.0

The long thought finds its full stops.

## Run-on sentences become sentences

Talk for a long stretch without stopping and the transcriber writes
one enormous sentence, a comma at every breath and never a full stop.
Now, when a long sentence pauses before "and", "but" or "so" and a new
thought begins, that pause becomes the full stop you meant. It happens
instantly, by rule, with nothing waiting on a model, and it is careful:
"calm and serene" stays together, "because" keeps its comma, short
sentences keep their spoken rhythm, and a sentence built on "if" or
"while" is never cut apart.

## Spoken words are safe from your contacts

"Gotta", "gonna", "wanna" and their friends can no longer be swapped
for a contact's name when the microphone is unsure. Dictation is
speech, and the spoken register is now protected the same way everyday
written words already were.

# Chalant 1.24.2

The wait keeps its word.

## Your words land on time, every time

When you let go of Option, Chalant waits a short, fixed moment for the tidied
version of what you said, and lands it once. That moment could occasionally
stretch while the rest of the machinery was busy; now the deadline is
enforced above everything else, so the wait can never be longer than
promised. If tidying is not ready in time, your words land exactly as you
said them, immediately, and stay.

# Chalant 1.24.4

The first press hears.

## A microphone has to prove itself

With earphones plugged in, a Mac's built-in mic can keep "delivering" pure
silence, and the first dictation after a rest could land on it and hear
nothing. At every press the microphone now has to lift the meter within a
second or it is swapped for one that hears, mid-hold, before your words
depend on it. And Chalant's automatic choice will never land on a virtual
device like Teams or Zoom audio; if you pick one yourself, your pick wins.

## No napping mid-sentence

macOS slows a background app's clocks to save power, and Chalant is in the
background whenever you dictate into another app. A dictation now runs under
a latency-critical declaration from key-press to landed words, so the tidy
pass keeps its budget wherever you're typing.

# Chalant 1.25.4

What you said is never lost.

## Handed-back words are keepers

When your dictation has nowhere to land, the cursor on the desktop, focus
that moved while you spoke, an app that would not take the paste, your
words now become a real copy: they appear at the top of the island's
Clipboard tab and stay there, even after you copy other things. The little
flash tells you where to look: "What you said is in your clips." Normal
dictation adds nothing to your clips, and anything spoken while a password
field held the keyboard stays out of the visible history on purpose.

# Chalant 1.25.1

The wait can no longer be stretched.

## The budget answers to a harder clock

When you release Option, Chalant waits a short fixed moment for your tidied
words and lands them once. That moment is now kept by a timer the rest of
the system cannot delay, however busy the models are, so a slow tidy can
never hold your words hostage: they land on time, as said, and the tidying
that finishes late simply feeds the ear's later correction instead.

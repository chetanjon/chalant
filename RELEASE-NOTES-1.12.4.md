# Chalant 1.12.4

The mic is back on screen. There has not been one since 1.10.0, while
the welcome tour went on telling every new person to tap it.

## Voice has a button again

It sits at the right of the tab row, next to the gear, and it is there
whichever tab the island opens on. Click it and the island listens.
Click again, anywhere on the listening panel, and it runs what you
said.

Holding the island still works and always has. That was the whole
problem: it was the only way left, and a press and hold is not
something anyone finds by accident.

## How it went missing

Two changes, a week apart, each sound on its own.

On 2 August the mic left the now playing row, where it had been sitting
beside the Spotify controls with nothing to do with them. Voice was
still reachable three other ways, and the code said so in a comment.

On 10 August the agent sessions surface was hidden. One of those three
ways lived inside it and went with it. Of the two left, the keyboard
shortcut ships bound to nothing until you set it, and the other is the
press and hold.

So from 1.10.0 on, a new install had exactly one way into voice and
nothing on screen that mentioned it. Someone who downloaded Chalant
this week reported the app as having no dictation at all, which was the
fair reading of what they were looking at.

## The listening panel stops asking for something you cannot do

It said RELEASE TO RUN to everyone, including anyone who had started
listening from the keyboard shortcut and had nothing to release. It now
says TAP TO RUN when you tapped, and keeps RELEASE TO RUN for the hold.

## Nothing else changed

No permissions moved. Recognition is the same Apple standard dictation
it was, and if voice worked for you before, it works exactly as it did.

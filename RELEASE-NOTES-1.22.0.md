# Chalant 1.22.0

The tour teaches dictation.

## A card for the second door

The welcome tour now has a card called "Hold Option and talk." on Macs that
can dictate (macOS 26). It names the gesture, says what your words get on the
way, and offers to turn hold-to-dictate on right there. Turning it on is what
asks macOS for Input Monitoring and Accessibility, so nothing is asked of a
Mac that only wanted the island. Show the welcome tour again is in Settings if
you want to see it.

## The site shows it too

The Chalant page has a section for it now, with a short film of a real hold on
this Mac: the strip under the island, the sentence landing tidy, the names
right.

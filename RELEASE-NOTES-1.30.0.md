# Chalant 1.30.0

The groundwork release: cleanup learns to keep pace.

## Cleaning keeps up with your voice

Chalant cleans your dictation a piece at a time, and a piece now
closes the moment you finish a sentence instead of waiting for forty
words. Behind the scenes, that means each sentence can be studied
while you are already speaking the next one, so when you release the
key there is almost nothing left to do. This release lays that ground
quietly; the switch that turns the full effect on for everyone is
being tested against real dictation and comes next.

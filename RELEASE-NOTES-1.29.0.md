# Chalant 1.29.0

Cleanup now does no harm.

## Your contacts stay out of your sentences

When the microphone left a word unclear, a contact whose name sounded
close could slip into its place: "all of that" once landed as "all of
Thota". No longer. Everyday English words are never rewritten into
names on sound alone, however unsure the microphone was. Teaching
still works exactly as before: correct a name in your document and
Chalant learns the fix from you, not from a guess.

## A pause no longer welds two sentences together

When a "like" or a "you know" is removed between two thoughts, the
comma that marked the boundary now survives: "on the top, like, we
need to improve" comes back "on the top, we need to improve" instead
of running the clauses together. Where the filler sat inside a single
thought, both commas still leave with it, keeping your sentences as
sparse as you asked.

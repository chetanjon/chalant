# Chalant 1.39.0

Your names, your long thoughts, and a quieter way to see it listening.

## The names you say are the names it writes

Chalant was refusing to fix a name that sounded close but not exact, so
your own contacts came back mangled. It now trusts a closer match,
because a separate safeguard already makes sure it can never rewrite an
ordinary English word into a name by mistake. Say a contact's name and
it lands right.

## Long unbroken thoughts become sentences

Talk for a long stretch without pausing and Chalant now gives you the
full stops you meant, even when you never breathe long enough for a
comma. It stays careful: sentences built on "if" or "while" are left
whole, and it never splits a phrase in the wrong place.

## It listens from the edge of the screen

The little strip that used to pop up while you talk is gone. In its
place, a soft light gathers along the very top edge of the screen,
brightening with your voice and fading when you stop. Nothing floats,
nothing covers your work.

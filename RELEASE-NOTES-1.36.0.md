# Chalant 1.36.0

Your contacts stop eating ordinary words.

## If the engine wrote a real word, Chalant believes it

A name from your contacts could still slip into a sentence when the
microphone was unsure and the word sounded close: "can you read this
chart" came back "can you read this Sharat". Chalant used to guard
against that with a list of everyday words, and a list can never hold
all of English.

Now it asks macOS. Any word your Mac's own dictionary recognises is
left exactly as it was heard, however close a contact's name sounds.
Unusual names are still repaired the way they always were, because
those are not words in anybody's dictionary.

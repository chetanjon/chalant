# Chalant 1.19.1

The drop tray moved up.

## Drops land under the island

When you drag a file or a link toward the notch, the "Drop to stash" tray now
hangs right under the island at the top of the screen, low and out of the way,
instead of appearing in the middle of your window. It shows only when a drag
is actually rising toward the island, so moving a tab or a text selection
across your work no longer summons it.

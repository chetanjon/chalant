Notes privacy

Applies to the Notes app for iPhone. Last updated 5 September 2026.

Notes collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party code inside the app. The developer cannot read your notes and has no server that could.

Where your notes live

  • On your iPhone. Every note is stored in the app's own storage on the device.
  • In your iCloud, if you are signed in. Notes syncs between your own devices through iCloud, using Apple's CloudKit private database. That database belongs to your Apple Account, is encrypted by Apple, and is readable only by your devices. The developer has no access to it. If you are not signed into iCloud, nothing leaves the phone.
  • On the Lock Screen widget. When you pin a note, a copy of its title and first line is written to a storage area shared between the app and its widget, on the phone only, so the widget can show it. Unpinning removes it.

What the app never does

  • Send your notes, or anything about how you use the app, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Ask for your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other permission.
  • Track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note in the app deletes it from the phone and from iCloud on your next sync. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that phone. To remove what is in iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and delete Notes from the list.

Children

The app has no content of its own and collects nothing, from anyone of any age.

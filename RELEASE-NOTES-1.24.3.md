# Chalant 1.24.3

Fast and refined are the same thing again.

## Your words come first, literally

Chalant's three listeners share one Neural Engine, and since 1.24.0 the
correcting ear was taking its turn at exactly the wrong moment, which made
the tidy pass miss its budget and your words land unrefined. The order is
law now: tidying owns the engine until your words have landed; the second
ear takes its turn after, and still covers your longest paragraphs.

## Said it twice? It lands once

Repeat a sentence inside one dictation, exactly, and only one copy lands,
whatever else is going on. Rephrasings are still judged carefully; emphasis
like "Yes. Yes." stays yours.
